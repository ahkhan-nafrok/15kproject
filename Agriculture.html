Red Chilli Exports — Guntur, Andhra Pradesh

Our red chillies are sourced from Guntur, Andhra Pradesh — a region internationally recognized as one of the world's premier chilli production zones. Guntur chillies are known globally for their vibrant colour, intense heat, and distinctive flavour profiles, making them highly sought after by food processors, spice manufacturers, and culinary supply chains worldwide. We supply multiple popular and internationally traded varieties — Byadgi (Bayadgi), Teja, and 334 — based on customer preference, heat level requirement, and end-use application.

Byadgi chillies are prized for their deep red colour and moderate heat level, making them the preferred variety for colour extraction, paprika production, and premium food colouring applications. Teja chillies are known for their very high pungency and heat content, widely used in spice processing and hot sauce manufacturing. The 334 variety offers a balanced profile suitable for general spice blending and food processing. All varieties are selected and prepared with strict focus on proper cleaning and sorting, uniform colour and quality grading, suitable moisture content control, and appropriate packing as per export specifications.

Onion Exports — Nashik & Madhya Pradesh

BNB Enterprises exports onions sourced primarily from Nashik, Maharashtra — India's most prominent and internationally recognized onion growing region — as well as from Madhya Pradesh, another major producing state. Nashik onions are known worldwide for their consistent quality, excellent shelf life, uniform colour, and keeping properties that make them ideal for export markets requiring product that remains in good condition through shipping and retail distribution. Madhya Pradesh onions complement our supply with similar export-grade quality and good keeping characteristics.

All onions are carefully graded by size and quality before dispatch to ensure uniformity and better shelf life. Our export process includes size grading as per buyer specifications (small, medium, or large), thorough quality inspection and sorting to remove damaged or substandard product, proper packing for transport and export — including mesh bags, jute bags, or carton packing as required — and timely dispatch to meet market schedules and seasonal supply windows.

Frequently Asked Questions About Spices & Agricultural Exports

Q.1 What red chilli varieties does BNB Enterprises export?

Ans. BNB Enterprises exports three of India's most internationally traded red chilli varieties from Guntur, Andhra Pradesh: Byadgi (Bayadgi), Teja, and 334. Byadgi chillies are celebrated for their deep, vibrant red colour and moderate heat, making them ideal for paprika production, colour extraction, and premium food colouring applications. Q.2 Why is Guntur, Andhra Pradesh significant for red chilli sourcing?

Ans. Guntur, Andhra Pradesh is internationally recognized as one of the world's most important red chilli production regions. Guntur is home to Asia's largest chilli trading market, drawing buyers from across India and internationally. Sourcing from Guntur ensures that BNB Enterprises' red chilli exports carry the authenticity, quality consistency, and variety purity that international spice buyers, food processors, and flavour manufacturers rely on.
