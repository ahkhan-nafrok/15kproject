Frequently Asked Questions for Alaska Red Granite

Q.1 What are the key factors of Alaska Red Granite?

Ans. Alaska Red Granite is well-known for its Vibrant Red with Pink COLOR_DESCRIPTION Grey, elegant appearance, exceptional longevity, resistance to scratches & heat, low maintenance requirements, and its capability to enhance the aesthetic appeal of any interior or exterior space with a unique natural look.

Q.2 What are the different uses of Alaska Red Granite?

Ans. It is usually used for elegant flooring, kitchen countertops, bathroom vanity tops, wall cladding, staircases, tabletops, outdoor paving, monument installations, and decorative applications. It is suitable for both residential and commercial interior & exterior décor projects.

Q.3 Which are the different finishes of Alaska Red Granite?

Ans. There are some different finishes of this granite such as polished (high gloss shine), honed (matte smooth finish), leathered (textured surface), brushed (slightly rough texture), flamed (rough outdoor finish), antiqued, bush hammered, and shot blasted finishes for various aesthetic and functional requirements.

Q.5 How to maintain Alaska Red Granite?

Ans. Clean regularly with pH-neutral stone cleaners and avoid acidic substances like lemon juice or vinegar. Seal the granite periodically to protect against stains. Use coasters under glasses and avoid dragging heavy objects across the surface to prevent scratches and maintain its natural beauty for years.
