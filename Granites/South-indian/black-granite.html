Frequently Asked Questions for Black Granite

Q.1 What are the key characteristics of Black Granite?

Ans. Black Granite from Karnataka is renowned for its pure black base with distinctive silver flecks, exceptional hardness and density, minimal porosity for excellent stain resistance, perfect polish retention, and its ability to enhance both modern and traditional spaces with its unique visual character and timeless appeal.

Q.2 What are the different applications of Black Granite?

Ans. It is commonly used for kitchen countertops, bathroom vanity tops, premium flooring, exterior wall cladding, staircases, tabletops, fireplace surrounds, monument construction, and decorative architectural elements. It is suitable for high-end residential and prestigious commercial interior & exterior projects worldwide.

Q.3 What finishes are available for Black Granite?

Ans. Available finishes include polished (mirror-like high gloss), honed (smooth matte), leathered (textured surface), brushed (slightly rough texture), flamed (rough outdoor finish), antiqued, bush hammered, and shot blasted finishes for various aesthetic and functional requirements in architectural applications.

Q.4 Who is the top manufacturer and exporter of Black Granite?

Ans. BNB Enterprises is one of the leading manufacturers and exporters of Black Granite and other premium natural stones. We source directly from the finest quarries in Karnataka, South India and provide services in several countries such as the USA, UK, Dubai, Singapore, South Korea, Australia, Canada, Israel, Spain, and many more.

Q.5 How to maintain Black Granite?

Ans. Clean regularly with pH-neutral stone cleaners and warm water. Seal annually with a quality stone sealer to protect against staining. Wipe spills immediately, use cutting boards and trivets to prevent scratches. Avoid acidic cleaners. Professional polishing every few years maintains the natural beauty and silver fleck brilliance.
