Kadappa Black Limestone

Premium Natural Limestone from Kadappa, Andhra Pradesh, India

Product Description

Kadappa Black Limestone is a luxurious natural stone exuding timeless elegance through its deep black hues and fine-grained smooth surface. Sourced exclusively from the Kadappa district of Andhra Pradesh, this prestigious limestone is renowned for its exceptional density, hardness, and polishability. The striking dark tones create dramatic contrast and sophisticated luxury, making it the premier choice for high-end residential flooring, commercial lobbies, statement staircases, contemporary wall cladding, and prestigious architectural installations worldwide.
