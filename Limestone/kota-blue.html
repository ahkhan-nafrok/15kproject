Kota Blue Limestone

Premium Natural Limestone from Kota, Rajasthan, India

Product Description

Kota Blue Limestone is one of India's most prestigious natural stones, renowned for its striking blue-grey tones and exceptional hardness. Sourced from the Kota region of Rajasthan, this premium limestone features a fine-grained compact texture that delivers outstanding durability. Ideal for high-traffic commercial flooring, outdoor pathways, pool surrounds, and grand entrance lobbies, Kota Blue offers a cool, sophisticated aesthetic that remains timelessly elegant in both modern and traditional architecture.
