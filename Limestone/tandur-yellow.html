Tandur Yellow Limestone

Premium Natural Limestone from Telangana, India

Product Description

Tandur Yellow Limestone is a timeless natural stone celebrated for its warm golden-yellow tones and subtle earthy texture. Quarried from the Tandur region of Telangana, India, this premium limestone offers excellent durability and versatility. Its warm hues and fine grain make it a preferred choice for traditional flooring, exterior paving, courtyard installations, and heritage restoration projects, bringing a classic sun-kissed elegance to every space.
