Ans. We offer five premium finishes expertly crafted by our skilled artisans: Polished finish provides glossy, refined surface ideal for interior flooring and feature walls. Leathered finish provides subtle texture with natural feel for modern applications. Natural Cleft finish preserves the stone's authentic texture for traditional and heritage projects, each finish revealing the limestone's unique characteristics.
