Q.2 What marble finishes does BNB Enterprises offer?

Ans. We offer four premium finishes: Polished finish provides a glossy, reflective surface bringing out natural colors, ideal for countertops, flooring, and wall cladding. Leathered finish offers textured appearance with slight sheen, hiding fingerprints better, perfect for countertops and vanities. Flamed finish creates rough, textured, slip-resistant surface for outdoor applications like pool surrounds. Lepatura/Antique finish delivers a unique granular worn-out antiqued look with rustic charm, non-slippery and easy to clean. Each finish is crafted using advanced equipment to reveal the marble's unique characteristics.
