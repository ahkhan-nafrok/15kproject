Frequently Asked Questions About North Indian Granites

Q.1 What makes North Indian granite different from South Indian granite?

Ans. North Indian granite is typically characterized by warmer color tones including pinks, reds, yellows, and creamy whites, with bold patterns and larger crystal structures. They are sourced from Rajasthan and surrounding regions with different geological formations. North Indian granites like Alaska Pink and Jhansi Red are known for their vibrant colors and are particularly popular for decorative applications, while maintaining excellent durability for commercial use.

Q.2 Which North Indian granite is best for kitchen countertops?

Ans. Alaska White, Azul White, and Desert Brown are excellent choices for kitchen countertops due to their subtle patterns that hide minor stains and scratches well. Alaska Pink and Jhansi Red add vibrant character to modern kitchens. All these varieties offer high hardness, heat resistance, and low porosity when properly sealed. Crystal Yellow provides a unique warm ambiance, while Copper Silk offers a sophisticated metallic sheen perfect for luxury kitchens.

Q.3 Are North Indian granites suitable for outdoor applications?

Ans. Yes, North Indian granites are highly suitable for outdoor use due to their exceptional weather resistance and low water absorption rates. Varieties like Desert Brown, Alaska Red, and Jhansi Red are perfect for exterior cladding, paving, landscaping, and monument construction. They withstand extreme temperature variations, UV exposure, and moisture without fading or deteriorating. Flamed or bush hammered finishes are recommended for outdoor flooring to provide slip resistance.

Q.4 How do I maintain and care for North Indian granite surfaces?

Ans. Clean daily with mild soap and water, avoid acidic cleaners that can etch the surface. Seal countertops every 6-12 months to prevent staining, especially for lighter varieties like Alaska White. Use cutting boards and hot pads to prevent scratches and thermal shock. Wipe spills immediately, particularly oils and acidic substances. For deep cleaning, use pH-neutral stone cleaners. Professional polishing every few years maintains the original luster.

Q.5 What are the most popular North Indian granite varieties for commercial projects?

Ans. For commercial projects, Alaska White and Desert Brown are extremely popular due to their neutral tones and ability to complement various design schemes. Jhansi Red and New Imperial Red are favored for hotel lobbies and corporate entrances creating bold impressions. Crystal Yellow adds warmth to retail spaces, while Black Marcino provides sophisticated elegance for luxury commercial interiors. These varieties offer excellent durability for high-traffic areas.

Q.6 Can BNB Enterprises provide custom sizes and finishes for North Indian granite?

Ans. Absolutely! BNB Enterprises specializes in custom fabrication of North Indian granites. We offer gangsaw slabs (280 x 180 cm), cutter slabs (180 x 60 cm), and custom tile sizes. Available finishes include polished (high gloss), honed (matte), flamed (textured outdoor), leathered (contemporary), brushed (rustic), and bush hammered. We also provide edge profiling, waterjet cutting for intricate designs, and thickness customization from 2 cm to 20 cm based on application requirements.
