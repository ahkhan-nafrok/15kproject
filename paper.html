Paper Scrap Import, Processing & Bale Supply by BNB Enterprises

BNB Enterprises is engaged in the import, local sourcing, and processing of paper scrap for the recycling industry. As part of a diversified global trading group with three decades of family business heritage, we bring the same rigour for quality, consistency, and reliable supply to our paper recycling operations as we do across all our trading verticals. We import paper bales from international markets and source paper scrap locally, supplying well-prepared, correctly graded bales to recycling units and paper mills across India and beyond.

Our focus is on proper grading, consistent quality, and reliable bale preparation to meet the exacting requirements of the recycling industry and paper manufacturing sector. From internationally graded import categories including OCC, SOP, ONP, OMP, and CBS to locally sourced mixed paper and printed book scrap, we handle a comprehensive range of paper scrap grades. Each grade is handled with strict attention to sorting, cleanliness, moisture content, and bale uniformity to ensure that recycling units and paper mills receive material that is immediately ready for processing.

All material is checked, sorted, and compressed into standard bales at our processing facility, with bale weights ranging from 1 ton to 1.75 tons — suitable for efficient transport, warehousing, and direct input into recycling and mill operations. By supplying consistent, clean paper scrap bales, BNB Enterprises supports both cost-effective paper manufacturing and the broader goal of environmental sustainability and circular resource use.

Frequently Asked Questions About Paper Scrap Recycling

Q.1 What paper scrap grades does BNB Enterprises handle?

Ans. BNB Enterprises handles a comprehensive range of paper scrap grades including Kraft Paper Scrap, OCC (Old Corrugated Containers), White Record/White Ledger, Mixed Paper, SOP (Sorted Office Paper), ONP (Old Newspapers), OMP (Old Magazines), CBS (Coated Book Stock), and Printed Book Scrap. We both import internationally graded paper bales and source paper scrap locally, offering recycling units and paper mills a single, reliable source for multiple grade requirements.

Q.2 What bale specifications does BNB Enterprises produce?

Ans. Our paper bales are produced with standard bale weights ranging from 1.0 to 1.75 tons per bale, suitable for efficient handling, transport, and direct input to recycling facilities and paper mills. Our bale preparation process focuses on proper segregation of paper grades, clean and compact baling using industrial baling equipment, uniform bale weights within each batch, and easy handling for transport and recycling plants. All material is checked, sorted, and carefully handled to ensure it meets recycling and mill specifications before baling.

Q.3 Does BNB Enterprises import paper scrap or source it locally?

Ans. BNB Enterprises does both. We import paper scrap bales from international markets, covering internationally recognized grades such as OCC, SOP, ONP, OMP, CBS, Kraft, and White Ledger. In addition, we also source paper scrap locally — including mixed paper and printed book scrap — and process it at our facility. All material, whether imported or locally sourced, undergoes the same rigorous sorting, grading, cleaning, and baling process before supply to recycling units and paper mills.

Q.4 Which customers does BNB Enterprises supply paper scrap bales to?

Ans. Our paper scrap bales are supplied to recycling units and paper mills where they are used as raw material for manufacturing new paper and packaging products. Our supply is suitable for a wide range of paper manufacturing applications including newsprint, corrugated board, packaging board, white writing paper, coated paper, and tissue paper — depending on the grade supplied. We are equipped to support both domestic paper mills and international buyers requiring well-prepared, graded paper scrap bales.

Q.5 How does BNB Enterprises ensure the quality of paper scrap grades?

Ans. Quality assurance at BNB Enterprises starts with careful selection of source material, followed by systematic sorting to remove contaminants, non-paper materials, and off-grade content. Each grade is segregated strictly and verified for purity before baling. Moisture content is monitored and maintained within acceptable limits for each grade. Bale weights are checked for uniformity, and finished bales are inspected before dispatch to ensure they meet recycling and mill specifications. Our decades of commodity trading experience means we understand what recycling units and paper mills require from their raw material.

Q.6 How does paper scrap recycling support environmental sustainability?

Ans. Paper scrap recycling is a cornerstone of the circular economy. By collecting, processing, and supplying paper waste back to paper mills as raw material, we reduce the need for virgin wood pulp, lower energy consumption in paper production, and divert paper waste from landfills. BNB Enterprises is proud to be part of this sustainability chain. Our well-prepared bales of high-quality paper scrap support cost-effective production for paper mills while contributing to environmental conservation and resource efficiency goals.
