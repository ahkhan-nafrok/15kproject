Q.2 What premium finishes does BNB Enterprises offer for South Indian granite?

Ans. We offer four premium finishes expertly crafted by our skilled artisans: Polished finish provides glossy, reflective surface bringing out natural colors, ideal for countertops and high-traffic applications like staircases. Leathered finish offers textured appearance with slight sheen, hiding fingerprints and watermarks better than polished granite. Flamed finish creates rough, textured, slip-resistant surface achieved by exposing granite to high temperatures, perfect for outdoor applications and pool surrounds. Lepatura/Antique finish delivers granular worn-out look with rustic charm, non-slippery and easy to clean.
